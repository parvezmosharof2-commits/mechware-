# B3 Credit settlement (receivables) — 🟨 B3.1 design, B3.2 + B3.3 ledger core, B3.3b payments/adjustments/planner, B3.4 record/verify/reject API, B3.5 bounce + ReduceChargeAsync, B3.5b apply on-account done (2026-11-16), B3.6 adjustments (2026-11-17), B3.7 overdue hold built (2026-11-17), B3.8 staff reads built (2026-11-17), B3.9 buyer reads built (2026-11-17, not committed), must have for launch
**Problem:** `Company.ReleaseCredit` exists but nothing calls it, so used credit never goes down and Net-15/30 customers lock up.
**Boundary:** credit ledger, recording customer payments against credit orders (cheque, pay order, BEFTN, RTGS, NPSB, deposit, cash, bKash/Nagad manual), maker-checker release, bounce, on-account money, adjustments/write-off, overdue aging + hold, buyer statement, reconciliation. Not gateway payments (E5), not invoices/credit notes themselves (E6), not cancel itself (E7.2).
**Design:** `docs/adr/0010-credit-settlement.md` · threat model §১২ CS1–CS31 in `docs/security/THREAT_MODEL_PRICING_QUOTES_PAYMENTS_INVOICING.md` (exit tests below are those names).
**Context pack:** the ADR (§ of the unit) · `backend/src/Mechware.Domain/Companies/Company.cs` (credit methods) · `backend/src/Mechware.Application/Companies/CompanyContracts.cs` · `backend/src/Mechware.Application/Orders/OrderContracts.cs` · `backend/src/Mechware.Infrastructure/Orders/OrderService.cs` (grep `ReserveCredit`: `SaveAsync`, `ApproveOrderAsync`) · patterns: `backend/src/Mechware.Domain/Payments/Refund.cs` (four-eyes), `backend/src/Mechware.Infrastructure/Persistence/StockMovementSql.cs` (append-only trigger), `backend/src/Mechware.Infrastructure/Workers/PaymentReconciliationWorker.cs`, `backend/src/Mechware.Api/Authorization/RequireStepUpAttribute.cs`, `backend/src/Mechware.Domain/Identity/Permissions.cs`.
**Rules:** every credit write goes through `Company` methods + a ledger row in ONE transaction; lock order orders → credit_payments/adjustments → companies → promotions; lock `orders` and `credit_payments` rows `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` on these paths (ledger foreign keys take `FOR KEY SHARE`; `FOR UPDATE` deadlocks, B3.5); settlement never locks `orders`; verifier ≠ recorder; no rounding (integer poisha); buyer reads = D2.10 404 rules but suspended companies still see their credit.

## Owner delegation, 2026-11-17

The owner delegated the open credit decisions ("tomar jeta valo money hoi koro"). Decided as follows; every one is configuration or a one-line rule, so the owner can still change it later:

- **OWNER 3-6 (credit options):** the built defaults stay (due date counts from confirmation, hold after 15 days past due, step-up on every verification, payment methods and limits as in `CreditOptions`, balance visible to Finance/Admin and the company's own owner).
- **OWNER 7 (payout wait):** cheque/transfer money is paid out only 30 days after it cleared (cash at once). Reason: a bounce cannot take back money already paid out.
- **OWNER 8 (hold override):** an Admin lift lasts at most 7 days and may be renewed; every lift needs a step-up code and a reason and is audited. No cap on renewals for now (an owner review of the audit rows replaces a second Admin); revisit when there is more than one Admin.
- **OWNER 9 (exports):** a single company's statement CSV needs `credit.view` and is audited and rate-limited, no code; the all-company aging CSV needs a step-up code.
- **B3.5 question (limit below credit used after a bounce):** the current rule stays: the limit cannot be set below what is in use, so Finance raises it to at least the amount in use.
