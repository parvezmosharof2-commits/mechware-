# D2 Price lists & contract prices — 🟡 (D2.1 ✅ design, decisions made 2026-11-13; D2.2 ✅ pure resolver; D2.3 ✅ aggregate + floor policy; next D2.4)
**Boundary:** price lists (company contract, shared group, public quantity breaks), validity windows, versions, 4-eyes approval with step-up, margin floors per brand/category, the price resolver and the price snapshot on the order line. Not inside: promotions admin (D3), quotes (D4, consumes the resolver and the floor policy), list/sale price editing (C1/C4).
**Resolution (brief §8.1):** the buyer pays the **lowest valid** of contract, group list, public quantity break, sale, list; the order contract > group > break > sale > list only breaks ties and names the source returned to the UI. An accepted quote (D4) bypasses the resolver. Decided (rule A).

## Decided (owner delegated to the main session, 2026-11-13; ADR-0008 §12)
1. **Rule A**, lowest valid price wins.
2. **Catalogue price is VAT-inclusive for everyone**; a company sees price ÷ (1 + VAT) plus a VAT line (D2.5 changes the two OrderServiceTests company-total expectations).
3. **Margin floor: 10 % minimum markup on cost, global, below-cost refused**; D2.4 seeds the one global row; the owner can change values and add brand/category rows later (D2.9). Permissions: edit = catalogue/sales, approve = finance/admin (never the author), approve_below_floor + manage_floors = admin.
4. **No group lists at launch** (D2.14 after launch).
5. 366-day maximum validity, 60-second activation delay.
6. Contract lists for MECHWARE's own store products only until the marketplace (H5).

Invalid entries (price ∉ 1…Max, minQty < 1, other variant) and lists with an inconsistent scope/company or other currency are ignored (fall back to the public price); bad requests (qty ∉ 1…10,000, catalogue price ∉ 1…Max, VAT ∉ 0…0.5) throw. `PublicUnitPriceMinor` = cheapest public candidate (list, sale, General break) for that quantity on the order basis.
