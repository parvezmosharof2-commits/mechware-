## OWNER decisions (ADR-0011 §15)
**Decided 2026-11-17 under the owner's delegation** (all are options or data; the owner can change them without a release): validity 15 days default, 1–90 allowed, > 30 days needs a second person · discount authority 5 % below the buyer's own price per line; more → Finance/Admin with a code; below the 10 % margin floor → Admin with a code and a reason per line; below cost refused · no quote-total threshold · quotes hold no stock · RFQs/quotes for verified companies only (personal/guest → inquiry form) · numbers MWQ-/MWR-YYMMDD-XXXXXX · PDF without digital signature or stamp, with preparer name and a verification code; no integrator-branded PDF · heavy items ordered through a fixed-delivery quote · one quote = one order, all lines; re-acceptance only after an order lapsed before confirmation · quote price fixed even when above the buyer's current price · payment terms "as account" or "advance only", never new credit terms · SLA clock Sun–Thu 09:00–18:00 Dhaka · approval sends the quote · e-mails link, never attach.
**Still the owner's (real data / legal; nothing waits):** (a) quotation terms-and-conditions text (lawyer); (b) letterhead facts: legal name, trade licence, BIN, address, phone, sales e-mail (= RFQ notice inbox); (c) public-holiday list for the SLA; (d) a real digital signature later (CCA certificate, provider, cost)?; (e) should named people (e.g. only the owner) approve below-floor quotes? (roles in G5).
