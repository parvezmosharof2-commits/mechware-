## 2. Vision, Mission & Core Values

### Vision
To establish Bangladesh's first advanced machine manufacturing plant to meet local industrial demand and reduce import dependency.

### Mission
Develop and manufacture high-performance industrial machinery locally, ensuring affordability, reliability, and technological independence for Bangladesh.

### Core Values
- **Innovation**: Explore modern technologies & better engineering.
- **Engineering Excellence**: Disciplined engineering for dependable performance.
- **Reliability**: Systems built for long-term operational stability.
- **Integrity**: Transparency, professionalism & responsibility.
- **Customer Focus**: Every project starts from the real requirement.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Modernize & optimize existing systems wherever value exists.

## 5. Technology & Product Ecosystem (E-commerce Marketplace Scope)

The MECHWARE platform will power both **B2B Industrial Machinery / Automation Storefront** and **Turnkey Engineering Service Inquiries**:

### Product Categories:
1. **PLCs & Automation Controllers**: Siemens (S7-1200, S7-1500), Mitsubishi (iQ-F, iQ-R), Delta, Omron.
2. **HMIs & Operator Interfaces**: Touch panels, industrial displays, smart operator interfaces.
3. **VFDs & Inverters**: AC drives, energy-saving industrial motor controls.
4. **Servo Systems & Motion Control**: Servo drives, precision servo motors, CNC controllers.
5. **SCADA & IIoT Gateways**: Web-based SCADA software, edge gateways, MQTT/OPC UA devices.
6. **Smart Sensors & Instrumentation**: Temperature, pressure, level, flow, optical, and proximity sensors.
7. **Industrial Control Panels & Switchgear**: Custom electrical control panels, circuit protection, relays.
8. **Made in Bangladesh Machinery**: Packaging machines, cutting machines, chemical dosing systems, ETP automation skids.
9. **Retrofit Kits & Spare Parts**: Replacement controllers, cables, encoders, pneumatic components.

### Engineering Services & Solutions (Quote / Inquiry Booking):
- Turnkey Automation Engineering & Commissioning
- Machine Retrofitting & Modernization
- SCADA & Cloud-connected Factory Dashboards
- Preventive Maintenance & Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)
- Industrial Technical Training
